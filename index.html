    Assumptions, definitions and public workflow references
    All brands, conversations, employees, costs, dates and outcomes are synthetic. The 50% Day 30 guarantee and the 70% Day 60 goal are distinguished. Guarantee eligibility and terms must be confirmed against the signed customer agreement. Aster’s guarantee window starts at confirmed customer-facing AI go-live. Readiness approval and channel cutover are recorded separately.
    The quality ledger uses mutually exclusive outcomes. Baseline, post-fix and Day 60 are separate completed 1,000-conversation samples. Their comparison is descriptive. The Day 30 guarantee view uses the full synthetic 30-day cohort. No-repeat-contact checks use a seven-day observation window. CSAT is shown with its response count.
    Brand support cost combines illustrative AI usage, human handling and the stated helpdesk allocation. AI cost remains on handed-off conversations. Richpanel internal delivery cost uses productive hours and a separate headcount budget. Capacity released is distinct from cash saved.
    Brand inspiration comes from Richpanel’s public website palette and typography. This independent simulation uses fictional policies, permissions and approval events.
